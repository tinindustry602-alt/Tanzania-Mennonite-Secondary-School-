About Our School

Tanzania Mennonite Secondary School is a private co-educational school located at Kisarawe II Ward, Ngoma Mapinduzi Street in Kigamboni Municipal Council, Dar es Salaam. The school offers Ordinary Level education for both boys and girls in a safe and disciplined learning environment.


The school is committed to provide quality education through excellent teaching, high discipline, practical training, and academic support. It also promotes English speaking programs, leadership skills, and moral values to help students become confident and responsible citizens.


In adition to academics, the school encourages participation in sports and extracurricular activities to develop students’ talents, teamwork, and healthy lifestyles. Tanzania Mennonite Secondary School continues to prepare young people for future success through education, discipline, and service to society.
